About

I’m Alice Croft, an artist based in Leeds specialising in reduction linocut and painterly printmaking. My work explores colour harmony, layered texture, and the quiet character of British landscapes and wildlife.

Available Work

Browse current pieces available for purchase or commission. Each print is hand‑pulled and signed. Visit my Shop page for details.

Process

My prints are created using a true reduction linocut technique — carving and printing each colour layer from the same block. This process demands precision and planning, resulting in unique, unrepeatable editions.

Workshops

I host small‑group lino carving workshops in Leeds, introducing participants to design, carving, and multi‑layer printing. Upcoming dates are announced on Instagram and through my mailing list.
