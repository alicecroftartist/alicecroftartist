Hi, I'm Alice! Here's a little background information about me.

I spent the last 14 years of my life training to become a GP, only to find that my real passion is making art. I've always made artwork as a hobby, but my professional career started early in 2026 when I bought an etching press second hand from a primary school. I really haven't looked back since, and I now love spending my days making beautiful prints.

My work primarily explores Yorkshire landscapes and the animals living within them. I have been a keen bird watcher for pretty much my whole life, and this has definitely shown through in my art practice. I like to spend my weekends walking in the Yorkshire countryside with my husband, who doesn't mind too much when I stop every two minutes to try and find an interesting bird that flew into a nearby bush. Sometimes, I think he might even be interested, and I am definitely converting him to be a fellow bird enthusiast!

My favourite printmaking technique is reduction linocut. I love the depth of colour that I can acheive and am a sucker for the challenge and jeopordy that comes along with knowing that you could ruin your print at any moment if it doesn't line up properly! I also experiment frequently with solar plate etching, where I have made architectural style prints of the beautiful buildings in Leeds.

Outside of art, I am a keen badminton player and also play underwater hockey!
