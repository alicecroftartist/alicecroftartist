About

Process

Reduction linocut

Reduction linocut printmaking involves using the same lino block to print multicolour prints. It is also known as suicide printing, as once you have carved the lino there is no going back! This process demands precision and planning, resulting in unique, unrepeatable editions.

Solar plate etching

Solar plate etching uses metal plates coated in photo-sensitive polymer. An image is drawn or printed on acetate and transferred to the plate using UV light. This process creates a variety of different finishes, from blocky relief prints to delicate photo reproductions.
