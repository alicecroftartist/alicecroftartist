Practice

My work combines traditional printmaking techniques with contemporary colour palettes. I specialise in true reduction linocut, building images through carefully planned tonal layers.

Reduction linocut Oil painting Colour‑driven

Inspiration

I’m drawn to Leeds architecture, markets, and quiet interior spaces. Many pieces explore light, shadow, and the way colour shifts across surfaces.

Leeds Architecture Atmosphere

Approach

Every print begins with a carefully sequenced palette. I plan each layer to keep transitions clean while allowing subtle tonal shifts that give the work depth.

Commissions & services

Original reduction linocuts

Limited‑edition prints created with a true reduction process. Ideal for collectors who value craftsmanship and colour.

Wedding & venue prints

Bespoke prints of wedding venues or meaningful locations. Designed as keepsakes or gifts for couples and families.

Includes: concept sketch, palette planning, final print.

Oil painting commissions

Commissioned paintings for homes, offices, or special occasions. We’ll work together on mood, colour, and scale.
